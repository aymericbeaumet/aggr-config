Incident

Trouble submitting workflow forms with file attachments on Android.

Resolved

This issue has been resolved. Android users who were experiencing issues submitting workflow forms containing a required file upload field can now do so successfully. A fix was delivered via a new update to the Slack Android app. Users who are still experiencing this issue should update the Slack app from the Google Play Store to resolve it. No other action is required. 

We apologize for the inconvenience and thank you for your patience.

9:48 AM PST

Investigating

We are aware that some users on the Slack Android app are unable to submit workflow forms that contain a required file upload field. After a file is selected, the Submit button becomes disabled and the form cannot be completed. Users on Slack for iOS and Slack for web are not affected. We are actively investigating the issue and working to identify the root cause. We'll provide an update in 60 minutes or sooner if additional information becomes available.
