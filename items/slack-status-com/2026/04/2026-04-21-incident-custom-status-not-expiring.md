Resolved

This issue is now resolved for all users.

- **Current status and recent actions taken:** Custom statuses set to expire at a specific time are now clearing as expected. The issue self-resolved when the next scheduled expiration job successfully processed the affected users.
- **Scope**: Some external users globally were affected. Users on desktop were impacted; the issue was not reproducible internally or across test workspaces.
- **Previous impact to end users:** Some users may have experienced custom statuses that did not automatically clear at their scheduled expiration time.
- **Required resolution steps**: No steps required.

We apologize for any disruptions to your day.

 8:47 AM PST

 Investigating

We're aware of an issue affecting Slack. Some users who have set a custom status to expire at a specific time are finding that their status is not automatically clearing as expected. We're actively investigating and we'll provide an update as soon as we have more information available.
