I’m a machine learning engineer with 20+ years of experience. I’ve worked at Airbnb and GitHub, including early LLM [research](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.09436) used by OpenAI for [code understanding](https://openai.com/index/introducing-text-and-code-embeddings). I’ve also led and contributed to popular [open-source](https://hamel.dev/oss/opensource.html) machine-learning tools.

I’m currently working to bring data science back to AI: helping teams debug, analyze, and measure their systems. I call this “evals,” and it’s the focus of my teaching and writing.

## 🎓 Learn With Me

I co-teach **[AI Evals for Engineers and PMs](https://maven.com/parlance-labs/evals)**, with over 5,000 students from 500+ companies (including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google). Check the page for upcoming cohorts.

For answers to common questions about evals, read the [AI Evals Guide](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/).
