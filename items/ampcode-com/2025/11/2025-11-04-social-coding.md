Update (2026-06-02): We've removed public thread sharing and related features; see [The End of Public Threads](https://ampcode.com/news/end-of-public-threads) for why.

We're all learning a new way to code: prompting, referencing, planning, handoffs, specs, and preparing a codebase to work well with agents.

Now, with public profiles and public thread sharing, you can see how other people are *actually* using agents to do real stuff. You'll probably learn something. We certainly have.

![Public profiles and profile cards for Amp users](https://static.ampcode.com/news/social-coding-pp-7.png)
