You can now pick the size of the orbs used to run Amp agents remotely. See [orb sizes and pricing](https://ampcode.com/manual/orbs#pricing) in the Amp Owner's Manual.

Go to [project settings](https://ampcode.com/projects) to change the size of a project's orbs.

We also doubled orb storage from 20GB to 40GB, at no additional cost to you.
