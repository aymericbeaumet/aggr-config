Amp can now require active [passkey](https://safety.google/intl/en_us/safety/authentication/passkey/) authentication for certain operations, such as [remote controlling](https://ampcode.com/news/neo#remote-control:~:text=walk%20through%20it.-,Remote%20Control,-When%20you%20start) a thread. This extra authentication factor protects you if an attacker gains access to your account and will serve as proof-of-human for future Amp features.

**Update (2026-06-25):** We've removed the "sudo" term and now just call it "passkey authentication".

To turn it on: [enable **Use Passkey Authentication** and set up your passkey](https://ampcode.com/settings/security).

Workspace admins can enforce this requirement for members. Also, some privileged workspace admin operations now *always* require active passkey authentication.
